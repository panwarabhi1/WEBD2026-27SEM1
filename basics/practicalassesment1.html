About Us

ABES Engineering College was established in the year 2000 under the Society for Educational Excellence. It is a leading private engineering institution located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh (Delhi-NCR). The college is affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow and is approved by AICTE. It holds NAAC accreditation and several programs are NBA accredited.

Vision: To be a centre of excellence in technical education that produces globally competent, ethical and socially responsible professionals.

Mission: To impart quality education through outcome-based learning, industry collaboration, research culture and holistic development of students.
